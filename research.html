Research

My research focuses on how capital is mobilised to decarbonise the economies that will drive most future emissions growth.

Private capital mobilisation for emerging economies

How public and private finance can be combined to fund the transition in emerging markets, including the role of national development banks, blended finance, and government borrowing from domestic institutional investors.

Hard-to-abate sectors

Steel, aluminium and cement are central to India's growth and to global emissions. This work covers the policy and financing options for low-carbon production, including carbon markets such as India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, state support, and green public procurement.

Trade and climate policy

How trade measures such as the EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) and steel tariff-rate quotas affect Indian exporters, and what the EU–India relationship means for climate finance and industrial policy.

Just transition

How finance can support workers and communities through industrial change, including the Just Energy Transition Partnerships and the design of disclosure rules such as India's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting.
