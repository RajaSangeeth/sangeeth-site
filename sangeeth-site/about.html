About

Sangeeth Selvaraju is a Policy Fellow at LSE, based in the Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment and the Centre for Economic Transition Expertise (CETEx). His work examines how the financing of industrial decarbonisation can be mobilised, with a focus on steel in India and other emerging markets.

Before joining LSE he worked in investment banking at Moelis & Company and in clean-energy advisory at Apricum Cleantech Advisory. He has also worked as a researcher at the Mercator Research Institute, at the Council on Energy, Environment and Water in New Delhi, and at DIW Berlin, where he led multi-country climate finance and nationally determined contribution (NDC) work across India, Indonesia, South Africa and Brazil.
